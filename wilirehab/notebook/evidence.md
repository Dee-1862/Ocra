## How to turn a row into text
1. Open the paper. Copy the sentence and the page number into the last column, in your own words plus a short quote under 15 words.
2. Change the status to `read`.
3. Write the paper sentence as: *[what the paper found]. We therefore [what our game or sensor does]* (the second half is ours; the first half must match the PDF).
